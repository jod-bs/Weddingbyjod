The JOD Story

Born from a belief that every love deserves to be celebrated with grandeur, Weddings by JOD has been the silent architect behind some of India's most cherished celebrations.

2018

The Founding Vision

Weddings by JOD was founded in Chennai with a singular vision — to elevate Indian weddings from mere events into curated, timeless experiences. Our first collection of 12 weddings set the gold standard.

2015

Going Pan-India

With an expanded team of bespoke designers, floral architects, and experience directors, JOD took its luxury brand across Mumbai, Delhi, Jaipur, and Hyderabad — becoming the go-to for elite celebrations.

2018

Award-Winning Excellence

Recognised by the Luxury Wedding Awards India as the "Best Bespoke Wedding Planning Company" three consecutive years. Our Rajasthan fort wedding for 1,200 guests became an industry landmark.

2021

The Digital Revolution

JOD pioneered the concept of the Digital Wedding Portal — a personalised, interactive website for each couple and their guests. This transformed how guests experience weddings even before arriving.

2024

Global Horizons

JOD expanded to international destination weddings — Bali, Dubai, Paris — while maintaining its deeply Indian soul. Today, we design 80+ weddings annually across 28 cities and 6 countries.
