The JOD Story

Born from a belief that every love deserves to be celebrated with grandeur, Weddings by JOD has been the silent architect behind some of India's most cherished celebrations.

2018

The Founding Vision

Weddings by JOD was founded in Chennai with a singular vision — to elevate Indian weddings from mere events into curated, timeless experiences. Our first collection of 12 weddings set the gold standard.

2015

National Recognition

Awarded "India's Best Boutique Wedding Planner" for three consecutive years. Expanded operations to Mumbai and Delhi, bringing bespoke luxury to India's most iconic wedding destinations.

2019

Going Global

Launched our Destination Wedding division, taking JOD's artistry to Bali, Dubai, Florence, and beyond. Every destination, one unwavering standard of excellence.

2022

Digital Innovation

Pioneered the Digital Wedding Portal — a bespoke, personalized digital experience for every couple and their guests. RSVP, live streaming, seating, guestbook, and memories — all in one beautiful space.

2024

500+ Celebrations

Crossed 500 weddings crafted across 28 cities and 12 countries. With a 98% couple satisfaction rate, Weddings by JOD remains India's most trusted luxury wedding platform.
